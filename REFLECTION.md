# Reflection & Ethics (Part H)

1. **Data Privacy Act of 2012:** The seller must obtain explicit consent, implement secure anonymization, and ensure strict access controls. We would remove or mask columns containing personally identifiable information (PII) such as customer names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses.
2. **COD Cancellation Finding:** Stopping Cash on Delivery (COD) entirely would hurt unbanked customers, regional buyers with limited digital access, and lower-income demographics. The seller should weigh logistics costs against lost revenue, perhaps introducing partial deposits or verified COD instead of a total ban.
3. **Big Data - 5 Vs:** 
   - *Volume:* The 1,000,000 simulated transaction rows generated for the 11.11 sale.
   - *Velocity:* The hourly traffic spikes (12:00-1:00 AM and 8:00-10:00 PM) simulating heavy checkout traffic.
4. **Internet of Things (IoT) in Supply Chain:** IoT fleet trackers and smart warehouse RFID tags could add real-time transit and temperature data to improve delivery estimates. Drawbacks include high infrastructure deployment costs and vulnerability to network outages.
